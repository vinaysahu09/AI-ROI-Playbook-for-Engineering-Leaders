Eleven spreadsheets, documents and slides that turn every framework in the book into something you can use on Monday morning. Free for every reader, whichever edition you bought. No sign-up needed.

## How the files work

- **Spreadsheets** open in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets or Apple Numbers. Type only in the pale orange cells; everything else calculates by itself.
- **Documents** open in Word, Google Docs or Pages.
- **Slides** open in PowerPoint, Google Slides or Keynote. Every shape is editable.
- **Example values** come from the book's illustrative worked examples. Replace them with your own before you show anyone.
